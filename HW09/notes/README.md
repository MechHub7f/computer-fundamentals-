# Research notes

`research-round-1.md` contains initial summaries, limitations, and leads. `model-and-handoff.md` documents the later alternate-history model, sensitivity checks, and verification priorities. The passages have not yet been visually checked against the source scans. This README is a template, not evidence of completed verification.

Keep substantive notes totaling at least twice the final paper's word count. For a 1,500-word paper, aim for at least 3,000 words of genuine notes, excluding templates and copied assignment instructions.

Suggested files:

- `source-summaries.md`: source-by-source summaries and relevant extracts.
- `investment-calculations.md`: prices, dividends, currencies, calculations, assumptions, and uncertainties.
- `counterevidence.md`: searches challenging the first draft, their results, and decisions about revisions.
- `revision-notes.md`: feedback and changes after reading the second draft.

### Relevant passages

Copy short extracts with exact page references. Clearly distinguish quotations, paraphrases, translations, and your own analysis. For non-English passages, retain the original alongside the translation.

### Summary and supported claims

Explain which claims each passage supports. Note qualifications and claims it cannot establish.

### Verification

Record which original pages you personally read and checked, including any uncertainty in OCR or translation. Do not mark a passage verified based only on a chatbot summary.

### Follow-up and counterevidence

Identify missing evidence, contradictory sources, and additional sources worth exploring.
